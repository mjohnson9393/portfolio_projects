# Video Game Sales Analysis (1980–2020)

**Tools:** Google Sheets (pivot tables, charts)
**Dataset:** [Video Game Sales](https://www.kaggle.com/) — Kaggle

## Overview
This project analyzes a dataset of 16,500+ video game sales records to explore
trends in the gaming industry by genre, platform, publisher, and time. The goal
was to practice real-world data cleaning and analysis skills while answering
specific, business-relevant questions.

## 1. Which genre had the highest total global sales?
Using a pivot table, I found that **Action games led all genres with 8,920.44
million** in global sales. This matters because it shows publishers and
platforms where to prioritize development investment.

## 2. How have total sales trended over time?
Global sales grew steadily through the 1990s and 2000s, **peaked around
2008–2009 (~680M)** near the height of the console era, then declined —
likely tied to the industry's shift toward mobile and digital distribution.

*Note: data coverage drops sharply after 2016, so 2017–2020 reflect
incomplete records rather than an actual sales collapse.*

## 3. Which platform and publisher had the highest total sales — and do they match?
**PS2 led all individual platforms.** However, **Nintendo led all publishers
overall.** This isn't a contradiction — Nintendo's sales are aggregated across
many consoles spanning decades (NES through Switch-era), while PS2's total
reflects just one console's lifetime.

## Key Takeaways
Action is the dominant genre by sales; the industry's growth peaked with the
console era before the shift to digital; and sales leadership looks different
depending on whether you measure by platform or by publisher.
